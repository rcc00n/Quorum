# Quorum portal — design system

Owner: Anastasia (portal look and UX, `anastasia/web-ux`), 2026-10-25. The identity itself is decided in [docs/DECISIONS.md › User account and interface](../docs/DECISIONS.md#user-account-and-interface) item 9; this file turns that decision into named tokens so pages use names, never raw values. Product and wording stay with Yelysei, the web platform with Vadim.

Direction, in one line (Anastasia's brief, 2026-10-25: newspaper aesthetics, a journalist's words about honest information, truth assembled from facts): a newspaper that shows its working. White paper, black ink, warm greys, one umber mark, rounded display type over a system body face. Calm and legible like Apple News; honest and inspectable like a trace.

Starting point (2026-10-25, Anastasia): Yelysei's prototype "Quorum — app screens" (Claude Design canvas, claude.ai/artifact/GxiEuhPNPSzH9SUjKjvYLX; sign-up → home → topic → story, plus "Also today"). Read in the browser, five frames. What it fixes: white page, rounded display face (Nunito-like) at about 56 px for page titles and 40 px on cards, a crimson date line above the title, grey secondary text, full-bleed photo cards with 20 px corners, white type and a small credit over the photo, "3 stories today" style one-liners, pill chips and a pill input, a light-grey rounded panel for "Also today", a dark near-black sign-up screen with a three-circle logo. Its screen logic predates the newest decisions (one edition a day prepared after the cut; topic search over existing names; the waiting state for a new topic; four rating modes), so the look is taken from it and the flows from DECISIONS.

Anastasia's references (2026-10-25, seven images: Peter Jellitsch's site, Les Garçons Sauvages, 5pm.fr, the MOMAT Takashi Homma poster, the Dutch Design Week "(In No Particular Order)" poster, RM's puzzle tracklist): black ink on white or cream paper, type as the picture, hairline rules and narrow columns like a printed page, a typewriter cadence (letters arriving one by one, a line rewriting itself), brackets and puzzle pieces as "truth assembled from facts", photographs small and documentary. Their grotesque typefaces are not adopted: the display face stays rounded per DECISIONS item 9 and the prototype; the newspaper structure, the rules, the columns, the typewriter motion and the bracket motif are.

Two of the reference sites were also opened live (2026-10-25): peterjellitsch.com sets everything in one grotesque at one weight, navigation at 60 px as the page's picture, body 18 px, black on paper; 5pm.fr the same idea in Neue Haas Grotesk at 17 px. The lesson kept: one display face, few sizes, type as the image; the grotesque itself is not adopted (item 9). lesgarconssauvages.com could not be opened.

What was compared besides it (2026-10-25, looked at in the browser, UXKIN not connected): the two sites Yelysei named as the quality bar, GreenTrace (greentrace-eudr.higgsfield.app) and Saphan (saphan.ai). Both are dark, one typeface each (Geist; IBM Plex Sans with Schibsted Grotesk headings), a 64–72 px headline that states the product's question, monospace eyebrows, and a real artifact as the hero visual: a verdict with its data, a trace walked step by step, a "do not trust it, check it" section. We keep the light identity already decided and borrow the structure: the real artifact up front, the loop steps numbered, each sponsor named by the job it does, limits said plainly.

No red in the identity (DECISIONS item 9, amended 2026-10-26); the prototype's crimson is superseded here.

## Spacing and layout

8-point scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128. Section padding 96 px desktop, 64 px tablet, 48 px phone. Content width 1120 px; reading column 38 rem. Side gutter 6% desktop, 24 px tablet, 16 px phone. Grid: 12 columns, 24 px gap.

Page measure (2026-10-26): on every route the content sits in a centred column of 1024 px including 22 px gutters (980 px of text), as on apple.com; the header's content, the landing's sections and footer, the app's main area and footer and the gate screens' body all read it (`--measure`, `--gutter`, `--column-gutter` in `web/app/identity.css`). A full-width bar keeps its full-width surface and hairline and only indents its content. Gutters are 16 px under 700 px. The landing hero gives six of its twelve columns to the text and five to the evidence sheet on this measure.

Landing rhythm (2026-10-26 evening): sections 128 px top and bottom (80 under 1000 px, 56 under 700), one object per section after a head of kicker, h2 and one sub line with 64 px under it; no hairline runs the page width (the kicker's ink rule and the section hairlines retired); the hero alone sits on `--measure-hero` 1200 px so its content starts 88 px outside the column at 1440. Type: h1 `clamp(48px, 6vw, 84px)`, h2 `clamp(38px, 4vw, 56px)` at 20ch, sub 20 px, step leads 24 px, body 17 px, mono meta 12 px.

## Shape and depth

Radius on the landing (2026-10-26, revised the same evening): `--radius` 8 px on containers (the evidence sheet, the anatomy page and its cards, the timeline's closing card, the trace ledger, the SQL block) and `--radius-s` 4 px on controls (buttons, chips, the focus ring); the 2 px of the morning is superseded for the landing and kept for the gate and app routes until their own change. A 2 px ink rule on top marks "a sheet of the product": the evidence sheet, the chain figure, the trace ledger. Depth: hairlines and a 2 px ink rule on the evidence sheet; no shadows; depth comes from `--paper-2` panels and hairlines. The header carries no rule and no shadow (2026-10-26, the double masthead rule retired): it is clear while the page rests at the top and frosted paper once the page scrolls under it.

## Motion

Transitions 180 ms ease-out on colour and transform only. Entrance animations 500 ms, once, on the hero. One typewriter: a headline that rewrites itself letter by letter (about 40 ms per character, a 1.6 s hold per line) over real titles only, with the finished line always present in the DOM for readers and screen readers. Everything off under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Components

- **Primary button**: `--ink` background, white text, 2 px radius, 48 px tall (42 in the masthead), 15 px 500, no arrow, no lift. Hover `--umber-ink`; focus ring `--focus` at 3 px offset so it sits on the paper around the dark button; disabled 50% opacity, cursor default.
- **Secondary button**: `--ink` text, 1 px `--ink` border, transparent background. Hover: `--paper-2` background.
- **Quiet link**: `--ink` text, underline on hover in `--umber`.
- **Kicker row**: a 1 px ink rule, the running head left in 12 px mono uppercase `--ink-2`, the section's committed source file right as a link (slug in lowercase). The hero's eyebrow is the same mono line without the rule.
- **Trace row**: monospace 13 px, three columns (node · decision · reason), hairline below, `--paper-2` background for the block.
- **Sheet** (the evidence card): `--paper`, 1 px `--rule` border with a 2 px ink top rule, 2 px radius, 24 px padding; figures at 28 px tabular, SQL behind a disclosure, then a Key: Value block in 12 px mono (Run, Window, Route, Datasource, Commit, File).
- **Spectrum** (sources; 2026-10-26 evening, replaces the tally grid): three equal columns, Left · Centre and not rated · Right, each with a head (mono label, the day's by-flank figure at 28 px, a 13 px line) and the registry's names sorted by the day's count, then major, then registry order; under each matched name a 6 px bar at count/max with 3 px corners, filled for full-text sources and outlined for headline-and-lead ones, in the cohort colour by flank and tier, ink in the centre; `headline` / `no feed` marks in mono after the name; — none matched, · no feed; a legend line; the full registry as a closed disclosure of mono rows. No interaction beyond the disclosure.
- **Anatomy page** (what you get): a 760 px bordered page with 8 px corners; a 120 px margin column of labels in grey parentheses, each slot the product's own rule from DECISIONS › Stories as articles; the Home and Also-today slots as `--paper-2` cards.
- **Timeline** (how it works): a 1 px rail with a 10 px dot per step (grey, ink on looping steps), a 240 px label column (mono number in grey, step name at 22 px, the node glyph line in grey, the vendor line in ink mono, a loop chip with the exact `loops.<key> = <n>`), a text column with a 24 px lead and a 17 px body; 56 px between steps; a `--paper-2` closing card.
- **Ledger** (the trace; replaces the trace table): a paper sheet with a 2 px ink rule, a head row with the fictional label and the row count, groups by part of the story in umber mono, rows of step number · node · decision in the text face with the reason beneath; loop rows carry a 2 px umber edge and "↺ back to <node>".
- **Field**: the day's by-source tally set as 60–120 px letterforms, blurred (SVG `feGaussianBlur` 26) in `--umber` at .18 and `--ink-2` at .14, masked to fade before the headline column; shown at 1000 px and wider only, behind the hero's sheet and beside the final statement; `aria-hidden`, every text node an id–count pair of the evidence file (tested).
- **Wire line**: the first row of the hero in 12 px mono: slug, edition and window, run time and commit.
- **Gate screens** (sign-in, sign-up, first setup; 2026-10-26, revised the same day): light by default, the landing's paper and ink, so the first form does not jump from a white landing to a black page; a theme switch in the header (mono label "Dark"/"Light", `aria-pressed`) turns on the dark version, kept per viewer in localStorage (`quorum-theme`) and applied to `<html data-theme>` before first paint. Dark values: `--night` ground, paper type, `--night-ink-2` secondary text (8.9:1), `--night-rule` hairlines, `--night-field` inputs, the button inverted (paper on ink), focus ring in paper. Both themes run through one rule set via `--g-*` custom properties in `web/app/gate.css`. Header: masthead left, the switch and the one alternative action right. Statement in the display face at 500 left, the form right; one column under 900 px.
- **Today board** (the daily brief; 2026-10-26, revised the same day): the page stays white; one section per followed topic, a hairline (`--rule`) above each, a display heading linking to the topic, a status in the body face and in ink (stories today, quiet day, waiting, or the hours to the cut), and one horizontal, snapping row of cards. Story cards: black at 60 % over the page with a 14 px backdrop blur (`rgba(20,20,20,.6)`; the blend reads as `#6a6a6a`, white type at about 5:1), 20 px corners, a reserved photo area at the top (16:9, 2:1 on the lead; it absorbs the spare height so the text sits at the foot of every card in a row) that shows a picture only when the snapshot carries a licensed one, with its credit, and an empty field at white 8 % otherwise; then the topic label in white 72 %, the display headline, the standfirst clamped to three lines, and the meta line (importance label · sources · minutes) in white 72 %. The first story of a section is the lead card, 680 px wide and at least 420 px tall with a 30 px headline; the others are 320 px wide and at least 320 px tall with a 20 px headline. The roundup card is paper with a hairline, the quiet-day and waiting cards paper with a dashed hairline, all in ink. Header: "Today" with the date in ink, the topic count, the status line, the date picker, "My topics". No section tints: only the cards carry weight.
- **Top bar** (all app routes; 2026-10-26, revised twice the same day): the former sidebar as the site header in the landing masthead's layout: 72 px, wordmark left ("Quorum", the dot hidden), Today · History · My topics centred with a 2 px ink underline on the current item, account and sign-out at the right, all in ink; the surface is the shared header's (clear at rest, paper at 80 % with saturate(1.8) blur(20px) once scrolled, no rule). The topic list of the sidebar is retired. On phones the menu takes its own scrollable line and the account name is hidden. The black 60 % bar of the morning is superseded; the story cards keep their 60 %.
- **Workspace** (daily brief, history, topic, event, settings; 2026-10-26, WEB-UX-04): `web/app/workspace.css` re-points `globals.css`'s `--accent`, `--bg`, `--text`, `--muted` and `--border` at the identity tokens and overrides the rules that hard-coded the former teal (sidebar, active nav, buttons, inputs, messages, the analysis views' `.r-*` chrome); headings in the display face at 500; one family throughout (the mono is not used on the app routes), all text in ink; the wordmark in the display face without the circle mark; 2 px corners. Cohort and status colours in the analysis views are data colours and stay.
- **Brief waiting state** (dashboard and topic page when no events are published; 2026-10-26): a 2 px ink rule, a mono kicker with the edition date and scope, a display-face sentence ("The 26 October 2026 edition is not published yet." or "… has not been cut yet."), the unchanged test sentence, a key–value block (edition cut as 05:30 New York, UTC and the viewer's clock, passed or in N hours; what happens next; whether the portal has any published edition), the waiting topics as hairline rows with a mono status, and a fine-print line about new topics starting thin. No count or date is ever shown unless the snapshot carries it.
- **Topic picker** (first setup and settings): a search field (`type="search"`, Enter adds the first match), the chosen topics as square chips with a remove button, the catalog as a grid of checkbox rows with hairline seams (two columns, one on phones), an honest line when the typed topic is not in the catalog, a mono footer with the count. Light defaults on paper, overrides on night.
- **Input**: 48 px tall, 1 px `--rule` border, 4 px radius, label above, error in `--umber-ink` below, said in words.
- **Header** (every route; 2026-10-26, revised): 72 px, brand left, links centre, primary action last; sticky; clear over the page while the page rests at the top, and once the page scrolls under it (html[data-scrolled], set by `components/scroll-state.tsx`) paper at 80 % with saturate(1.8) blur(20px) over 0.24 s (`app/header.css`); no rule and no shadow, the 3 px double masthead rule retired. Collapses to brand + button under 1000 px on the landing; the app bar keeps its menu on a second line.
- **Footer**: hairline, brand, one line, links.

## Icons and imagery

No icons on the landing (2026-10-26); the app routes keep Lucide, 20 px, stroke 1.75, `--ink-2`. Photos only where the product shows them (topic cards, stories) and always with a credit; the landing uses no stock photography and no illustration. Diagrams are the product's own artifacts: trace rows, cohort grids, the graph.
